# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

OpenTrip Engine is a community project for people building tools and workflows for travel businesses. We are committed to making participation welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing problems directly.
- Give actionable feedback and explain technical decisions.
- Welcome questions from first-time contributors.
- Respect privacy: never publish customer data, credentials or private information.
- Disclose security issues privately rather than posting exploitable details in public.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately through the GitHub profile: https://github.com/azeddinee

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews and other project-related interactions.
